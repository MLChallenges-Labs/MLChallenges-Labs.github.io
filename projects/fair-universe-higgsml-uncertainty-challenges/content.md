## The collaboration

The FAIR Universe project brings together scientists from Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, Université Paris-Saclay, the University of Washington, and ChaLearn, a non-profit that organizes challenges to stimulate research in machine learning. The project is funded by the US Department of Energy.

The initial focus is on discovering and minimizing systematic uncertainties in High Energy Physics, through a sequence of challenges that bring together researchers across physics and machine learning.

## The challenge series

### 1. Toy Challenge (October 2, 2023)

A first toy challenge was released on the Codabench platform at the start of the project, designed to test the platform and provide a simple example for the community to follow. A toy dataset was created to illustrate the types of analyses performed on real data from particle accelerators.

[Competition website](https://www.codabench.org/competitions/1152/)

### 2. Particle Physics challenge trial (November 29, 2023)

A hackathon was hosted at the *Artificial Intelligence and the Uncertainty Challenge in Fundamental Physics* workshop. FAIR Universe released a trial version of a challenge using detailed physics simulations of accelerator data.

[Competition website](https://www.codabench.org/competitions/1299/?secret_key=28d9c0fc-fe66-44c8-be89-0f2c712b4514)

### 3. HiggsML Uncertainty Pilot Competition (March 12, 2024)

A pilot version of the HiggsML Uncertainty Competition was launched for participants. This trial competition focused on involving participants to refine the competition by updating the task, dataset and metric in a feedback loop.

[Competition website](https://www.codabench.org/competitions/2164/)

### 4. The challenge of handling uncertainties in fundamental science (NeurIPS 2024)

The full challenge, *FAIR Universe: the challenge of handling uncertainties in fundamental science*, was accepted at the NeurIPS 2024 Competition Track and launched in August 2024.

[Competition website](https://fair-universe.lbl.gov/)
